Frequently Asked Questions

How do I make a deposit? +
Navigate to the 'Deposit' page, enter the amount (min ₦2,800), and follow the payment instructions. Your wallet will be credited once confirmed.
When do I receive earnings? +
Earnings are credited daily to your account based on your active investment plan. Each plan cycle lasts 20 days.
How do I withdraw funds? +
Go to the 'Withdraw' page, enter an amount (min ₦650), and provide your payout bank details. Processing takes up to 24 hours.
